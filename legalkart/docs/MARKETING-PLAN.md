# LegalKart Paper Generator: Marketing Plan & Launch Kit

**Scope:** go-to-market for the MVP (rent agreements, affidavits, NOCs and rent receipts in **Delhi NCR (Delhi, Gurugram, Noida) and Bengaluru**), then the Phase 2 expansion.

Everything in §7 is ready-to-use copy. Placeholders look like `[this]`.

> **Before anything goes out:** run the copy through the [marketing compliance checklist](#10-marketing-compliance-checklist). Legal-services marketing in India has stricter rules than most categories.

## 1. Positioning

**One-liner**
> Stamped rent agreements in 10 minutes, from your phone. No stamp vendor, no queue.

**Positioning statement**
> For **landlords and tenants in Indian cities** who need a rent agreement quickly, **LegalKart** is the **document app** that **calculates your state's stamp duty, buys the e-stamp online and gives you a ready-to-print agreement**. Unlike visiting a stamp vendor or using a generic template, **every agreement is matched to your property's pincode, the tenant's mobile is OTP-verified, and every page carries a scannable verification code.**

**Category:** online legal document preparation (*not* legal advice).

### Why people switch

| Old way | With LegalKart |
|---|---|
| Find a stamp vendor, queue, buy the wrong value | Duty worked out from your pincode, rent and deposit |
| Copy a template from the internet, unsure it fits your state | State-specific template, reviewed by an advocate |
| Typos, missing clauses, no record of who agreed | Guided form, tenant OTP verification, digital copy kept |
| Half a day off work | About 10 minutes on your phone |
| Can't tell if a paper is genuine | QR code on every page to verify it |

## 4. Pricing presentation

Show the price as three parts, always. This transparency is a differentiator.

| Line | Example (Gurugram, ₹25k rent, 11 months) |
|---|---|
| Stamp duty, paid to the Government of Haryana | ₹4,875 |
| LegalKart fee (incl. e-stamp vendor charge) | ₹249 + GST |
| **You pay** | **₹5,168.82** |

**Launch offers** (pick one, time-boxed):
- **Fee waived on your first agreement** (stamp duty still applies). This is the strongest acquisition hook.
- **₹100 off with a referral code**, for both the referrer and the new user.
- **Brokers:** 10-agreement pack at ₹1,990 + GST (fees only).

> Never advertise "free rent agreement" unless the stamp duty is also free. It isn't, and the claim would be misleading.

## 10. Marketing compliance checklist

Every external asset is checked against this list before it goes out.

- [ ] **No legal-advice claims.** Never say "legal advice", "lawyer-drafted for you", "100% legal", "court-proof", "guaranteed valid" or "government approved". *OK:* "advocate-reviewed templates", "genuine government e-stamp".
- [ ] **Advocate involvement is described accurately.** Bar Council of India rules bar advocates from advertising or soliciting work. Don't feature partner advocates by name or photo as a selling point without legal sign-off.
- [ ] **No implied government or SHCIL endorsement.** Don't use government emblems, the SHCIL/StockHolding logo or state seals. "E-stamp issued through authorised channels" is fine.
- [ ] **Prices are complete.** Any fee claim states that stamp duty is extra, or shows the total. No "free agreement" unless it is.
- [ ] **Time claims are qualified.** "About 10 minutes" is tied to the self-print e-stamp states; don't promise "instant" where stamps are issued manually.
- [ ] **ASCI code:** claims are substantiated, testimonials are genuine with written consent, influencer posts are labelled (#ad / #sponsored).
- [ ] **TRAI / DLT:** promotional SMS only from registered promotional headers and templates, within permitted hours, honouring DND preferences; never mix promotional content into OTP templates.
- [ ] **WhatsApp:** marketing messages only to users who opted in; an opt-out in every broadcast; use Meta-approved marketing templates.
- [ ] **DPDP Act:** separate, unbundled consent for marketing (not hidden in the OTP consent); honour withdrawal promptly. **Never market to tenants or other parties** whose numbers we hold only for verification, unless they sign up themselves.
- [ ] **Data in creatives:** use fictitious names, numbers and certificate numbers only. Blur real certificates and real addresses.
- [ ] **Disclaimer** on the landing page, app listing and long-form content.
